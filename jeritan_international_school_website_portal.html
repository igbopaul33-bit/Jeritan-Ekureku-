About Jeritan

Dedicated to Educational Progress in Cross River State

Jeritan International School was established in Ekureku, Abi Local Government Area, Cross River State, to provide top-tier primary and secondary education. Built on values of integrity, discipline, and modern academic standard, Jeritan caters to students from all communities in Cross River and beyond.
